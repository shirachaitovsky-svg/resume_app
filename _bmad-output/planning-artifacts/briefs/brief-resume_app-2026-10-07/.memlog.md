- (decision by user) The instructor wants a shared website for AI development course participants, presenting profiles, workplaces, answers to introductory questions, and links to products participants deploy to the cloud. CV upload is optional, not required. Audience, visibility, publication workflow, and number of cohorts remain open.
- (decision by user) Primary purpose: visitors see course products and are impressed by the professionalism and the participants who built them. Showcase and participant discovery are central; recruitment is not an established goal. Updated brief purpose, audience and success criteria.
- (decision by user) Confirmed public website with participant self-entry. Profiles remain non-public until explicit instructor approval. Instructor requires an admin screen to review pending participants and approve publication. Edit reapproval, rejection/corrections, and participant access remain undefined.
- (decision by user) User approved reapproval for edits to published profiles. Existing approved version stays public while changes await instructor review. Pending edits must not replace public content before approval; admin review includes profile updates.
- (decision by user) Website will serve the current course cohort and future cohorts. Brief now includes multiple cohorts with each participant profile associated with its course cohort. Cohort management and browsing details are not yet defined.
- (decision by user) Confirmed invitation link per cohort. Participants fill out and submit profiles directly on the website via that link, associated with the invitation cohort, for instructor approval. Authentication for returning to edit remains open.
- (decision by user) Participants will not have LinkedIn profiles. Remove LinkedIn from proposed profile fields; participant introductions rely on information entered on the website and their course products. Other proposed profile fields remain unconfirmed.
- (decision by user) Accepted three introductory questions: professional background; what problem the product solves and for whom; course learning already applied. All three are optional individually; participants may submit without answering any. Other field requirements remain open.
- (decision by user) Confirmed required profile fields: participant name, product name, product link. All other introductory profile information is optional, including job/workplace, introduction, participant photo, CV and introductory questions. No LinkedIn field. Authentication requirements are separate and remain open.
- (decision by user) Confirmed passwordless email-link login. Email and mobile phone are mandatory, visible only to instructor among other users, never public or visible to other participants; form must emphasize this. Public visitors may request contact details for a specific listed participant. No automatic disclosure; request routing, participant consent, requester fields and fulfillment remain undefined.
- (decision by user) Contact disclosure always requires explicit participant approval for the specific request. Publishing approval is not consent to contact disclosure. Form explanation must clarify this exception to admin-only visibility. Routing, requester fields, approval mechanism and delivery remain open.
- (decision by user) Contact-detail requests will appear in the instructor admin screen. Each new request also triggers an email notification to the instructor. Participant approval mechanism, requester fields and contact delivery remain open.
- (decision by user) Instructor can send participant an email from the admin screen containing contact-request details and a personal approval/refusal link. Participant response is recorded on the request for instructor visibility. Only explicit approval permits disclosure; refusal or no response does not. Requester fields and post-approval delivery remain open.
- (decision by user) Confirmed mandatory contact-request fields: requester name, email address and reason for contacting. Request is associated with the selected participant. These details are shown to instructor and participant before consent, not published publicly. Post-consent contact delivery remains open.
- (decision by user) Contact delivery requires both explicit participant consent and separate instructor approval for the same request. Participant consent alone does not send details. Once both approvals exist, contact details are automatically emailed to the requester. Missing either approval prevents delivery.
